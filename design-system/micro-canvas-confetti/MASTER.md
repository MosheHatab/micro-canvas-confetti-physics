## Demo UX rules
- Preset cards fire preset-only bursts (not overridden by sliders)
- Sliders labeled in confetti terms: Pieces, Size, Spread, Speed, Fall, Duration
- Short duration = quick end (~1s)
- Spark preset = micro 8-piece pop
